TriForge - Data Deletion Requests

TriForge is provided by Inside Out Creators.

TriForge does not require users to create an account. Calculator measurements are processed locally on your device.

How to request data deletion

You can request deletion of applicable personal information associated with TriForge licensing by emailing:

shane@insideoutcreators.com.au

Use the subject line: TriForge Data Deletion Request.

Please describe your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

What information can be deleted?

TriForge stores its offline licence receipt locally. Uninstalling the app normally removes this locally stored information and its associated app-specific cryptographic key.

We will review requests concerning identifiable personal information in licensing-related operational records that we control and delete eligible information where reasonably possible.

Some information may be retained when necessary for security, legal obligations, fraud prevention, or operational requirements.

Google Play purchase and payment records are managed by Google and are subject to Google's own retention and deletion policies.
