TriForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

TriForge is a geometry and measurement calculator designed for tradespeople, engineers, apprentices and other users.

This privacy policy explains how TriForge handles information when you install and use the Android application.

1. Developer and contact

TriForge is provided by Inside Out Creators.

For privacy questions, contact: shane@insideoutcreators.com.au

2. Calculator information

Calculations and measurements entered into TriForge are processed locally on your device.

TriForge does not require you to create a separate account or upload your calculations to our server.

3. Google Play licence verification

TriForge uses Google Play Integrity to verify application authenticity and Google Play licensing.

During licence verification, the application communicates with a licensing server hosted on Google Cloud.

The verification process involves an integrity token, a randomly generated request challenge and, where applicable, a device-generated public cryptographic key.

Google processes integrity information under its applicable privacy terms. Our licensing server uses the verification result to determine whether the installation has a valid licence.

4. Permanent offline licensing

Following successful online licence verification, TriForge can store a digitally signed licence receipt locally on the device.

The receipt is associated with a device-generated cryptographic key. The corresponding private key remains in Android Keystore and is not intentionally uploaded to our licensing server.

The stored receipt allows licence verification without an internet connection. A new installation or replacement device may require another online verification.

5. Information processed by our server

Our licensing server processes verification requests needed to check purchase entitlement and issue offline licence receipts.

Google Cloud infrastructure may also process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, request timestamps, response status codes and diagnostic logs, for security, service operation and troubleshooting.

We do not use licence verification requests to collect calculator measurements or build advertising profiles.

6. Advertising and analytics

TriForge does not contain third-party advertising or advertising trackers.

TriForge does not intentionally collect usage analytics through a separate analytics service. Google Play may provide the developer with aggregated installation, purchase and application performance information.

7. Purchases and payments

Purchases are processed through Google Play. TriForge does not directly collect or store payment-card details.

Google handles payment information in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms.

8. Information sharing

Data needed for licensing and app distribution is processed using Google Play and Google Cloud services. We do not sell users' personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

9. Data retention and deletion

Offline licence information is stored on the device. Uninstalling TriForge normally removes this locally stored app information and its associated app-specific cryptographic key.

Google Cloud may retain operational and diagnostic records according to the service's configured logging and retention settings.

To ask about personal information associated with licensing operations, or request deletion where applicable, contact the email address above. Certain records may need to be retained for legal, security or operational reasons.

10. Security

TriForge uses encrypted network connections for licence verification, Android Keystore for device cryptographic keys, and digitally signed licence receipts to help protect licensing information.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's privacy

TriForge is a general-purpose technical calculator and is not specifically directed at children.

12. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes in TriForge, its services or applicable requirements. Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact

For questions about this policy or your privacy: shane@insideoutcreators.com.au
